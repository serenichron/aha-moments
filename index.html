Meet Your Guide

Hi, I'm Liana

I believe every parent has the capacity for emotional clarity and connection — sometimes we just need someone to help us find it.

As a practitioner grounded in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) principles, I've dedicated my work to helping parents who feel emotionally overwhelmed, stuck in conflict cycles, or eager to break generational patterns.

I know what it's like to feel like you're failing at the most important job in the world. And I know the relief that comes when you finally understand why you react the way you do — and what to do instead.

My approach isn't about making you a "perfect" parent. It's about helping you become a more aware, present, and emotionally regulated one. One aha moment at a time.

The Transformation

What You'll Gain

After just two sessions, parents report feeling lighter, clearer, and more confident in their ability to handle challenging moments.

A Brighter Path Forward

Understand the "why" behind your emotional reactions. Discover your triggers and patterns, and gain insight into how your own experiences shape your parenting. This clarity is the foundation for lasting change.

Receive evidence-based techniques from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy that you can apply immediately. These aren't abstract concepts — they're practical tools for managing emotions in real-time, during real parenting moments.

Learn to pause before you react. Develop the ability to respond thoughtfully instead of impulsively, even when emotions run high. Feel more in control of your responses and less controlled by your triggers.

Walk away with a custom-written plan designed specifically for your family. Includes reflection exercises, journaling prompts, behavior observations, and simple daily practices that fit into your real life.

Gain awareness of how your own upbringing influences your parenting today. Take the first steps toward breaking cycles you don't want to repeat, and building the relationship you want with your child.

Experience what it feels like to be truly heard without judgment. Leave feeling empowered, validated, and more connected — not just to your child, but to yourself as a parent.
