Meet Your Guide

Hi, I'm Liana

I'm an emotional guidance practitioner grounded in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) principles. But more importantly, I'm someone who understands the weight of wanting to be a better parent while feeling like you're failing every day.

I created "Aha Moments" because I believe that emotional clarity changes everything. When you understand why you react the way you do, you can finally start responding differently.

My approach isn't about perfection. It's about progress, self-compassion, and practical tools you can actually use when your 4-year-old is having a meltdown and you're at your limit.

How It Works

1
Complete the Intake Form

A short questionnaire helps me understand your situation before we meet. It takes about 10 minutes and sets the foundation for our work together.

2
Session 1: Discovery & Clarity

We explore your emotional patterns, triggers, and the dynamics that keep you stuck. You'll leave with your first "aha moment" and immediate clarity.

3

Setting Expectations

What This Is (And Isn't)

This Program IS:
  • Emotional guidance rooted in CBT principles
  • A safe space to process your feelings
  • Focused on clarity and practical tools
  • Personalized to your unique situation
  • Designed for busy parents (minimal time commitment)
  • Judgment-free and compassionate
This Program IS NOT:
  • Therapy or clinical treatment
  • A quick-fix or parenting "hack"
  • One-size-fits-all advice
  • Deep trauma processing
  • A replacement for professional mental health care
  • Another course you won't finish
